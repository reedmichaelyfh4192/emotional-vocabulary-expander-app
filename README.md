## What the Tool Does

Emotional Vocabulary Expander is intended for anyone who wants a clearer way to describe what is happening internally. A starting point such as "I feel bad" can be examined through a guided process that helps narrow it down to language that more closely reflects the actual emotion.

The project is built for personal reflection and can support exploration of emotional identification, including experiences related to alexithymia. Along the way, it explains why identifying emotions may be helpful and presents ideas for reflection or possible next steps once a feeling has been named.

---

## Core Capabilities

- Turns broad descriptions of feelings into more detailed emotional possibilities.
- Leads users through a step-by-step process for identifying emotions.
- Encourages comparison between vague labels and more exact descriptions.
- Describes the potential benefits of building a broader emotional vocabulary.
- Invites users to think about what a particular emotion might be expressing.
- Offers possible directions for reflection after an emotion is identified.
- Runs in a web browser without a separate desktop runtime.
- Centers on emotional awareness and individual self-exploration.

## How to Use It

1. Enter or consider a general description of how you currently feel.
2. Work through the prompts to investigate that feeling further.
3. Assess the broader starting label against the more specific options.
4. Choose or record the emotion that most accurately fits your experience.
5. Read about the usefulness of expressing emotions more precisely.
6. Use the suggested reflection or next step as a prompt for further thought.

This project is for personal exploration and does not take the place of professional mental health care.

It is for people who want to examine their emotions and find more exact language, especially when a broad term does not capture the full experience.

No. The tool is described as a self-reflection resource for emotional identification and vocabulary, not as a diagnostic system.
